# Customer discovery: test the business before building more

The market check found that risk classification alone is free (EU Commission checker,
Future of Life Institute, TÜV SÜD). The paid pain is the **buyer questionnaire** an AI vendor
must answer before an EU enterprise deal closes. The Evidence Pack targets that, starting
with AI hiring / HR-tech vendors (Annex III point 4 is the clearest high-risk case).

## Two-week test

1. **Find 15 people**: founders, CTOs or heads of product at AI hiring / HR-tech startups
   (10-200 staff) that sell to EU customers. Sources: LinkedIn, HR-tech directories,
   authors of "we just got an AI Act questionnaire" posts.
2. **Interview about the past, never pitch.** Use the script below.
3. **Decide the stop rule now:** fewer than 5 of 15 have actually faced a questionnaire,
   or nobody has spent time or money on it → the pain is not sharp enough. Stop.
4. **Show, don't tell** (only in the last 5 minutes): run the sample pack on their public
   docs, or show `examples/evidence_pack.md`.

## Interview script (30 min)

Warm-up
- What does the product do, and which countries are your customers in?

The past (only real events count)
1. When did a customer last send you an AI, security or compliance questionnaire?
2. Did it mention the EU AI Act? What was question one?
3. Who on your team answered it? How many hours / days did it take?
4. Did it slow or block a deal? Which deal, how big?
5. What did you use to answer (docs, ChatGPT, a consultant, Vanta, spreadsheets)?
6. What was the most uncomfortable question to answer?
7. Did you get a follow-up from the buyer after you answered?

The money
8. What have you paid for so far (tools, lawyers, consultants)? How much?
9. Who owns this budget?
10. If this were solved, what would you do with the saved time?

Close
11. Who else should I speak to?
12. Can I send you a sample answer to your last questionnaire for feedback?

## What to record per interview

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Company / role | HR-tech, 40 staff, CTO |
| Faced a questionnaire? | yes, Q3 2026, German bank customer |
| Hours spent | 18 |
| Deal impact | 5-week delay on a mid-five-figure contract |
| Current tool | spreadsheet + outside counsel |
| Paid so far | EUR 4,000 legal review |
| Strongest pain quote | "we had no idea what to say about logging" |

## Signals that mean build further

- 5+ of 15 had a real questionnaire in the last 12 months.
- At least 3 spent money or 10+ hours on it.
- Someone offers to pay, pilot, or share a real questionnaire.

## Dealbreakers to watch for

- "Our lawyer handles it" with no pain → lawyers, not software, are the buyer.
- Buyers accept a one-paragraph answer → no real documentation burden.
- Existing tool (Vanta etc.) already used and liked → need a sharper niche.
