# Finding your first 15 interviews

I did not invent company names. Build the list yourself from real sources, then verify each
company sells in the EU before you contact anyone.

## Who to look for
AI-powered hiring and HR-tech vendors, 10-200 staff, with EU customers. Typical products:
CV parsing and ranking, interview scheduling with AI scoring, skills matching, video-interview
analysis, talent-pool search, internal mobility, workforce analytics.

Target title: founder, CTO, head of product, head of security/compliance, or whoever answers
customer security questionnaires.

## Where to search
| Source | Query ideas |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn people | `CTO "recruiting software"`, `founder "HR tech" Germany`, `head of product "AI screening"` |
| LinkedIn posts | `"AI Act" questionnaire procurement`, `"AI Act" "vendor questionnaire"` |
| Company websites | footers or trust pages mentioning "AI Act", "high-risk", "Annex III" |
| Directories | HR Tech Outlook lists, G2 and Capterra "applicant tracking" and "AI recruiting" categories, EU HR-tech conference exhibitor lists |
| Communities | HR tech Slack/Discord groups, r/recruiting, founder communities |
| Job posts | vendors hiring a "compliance" or "AI governance" person have the pain right now |

## Tracking sheet columns
Company, URL, size, EU customers (Y/N, source), contact name and title, channel, date sent,
replied, interview date, faced a questionnaire (Y/N), hours spent, deal impact, money spent,
notes. Mark a company only after you have checked its EU customers.

## Message templates (keep it short, no pitch)

**LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)**
> Hi {name}, I'm a student researching how AI hiring vendors handle EU customer compliance questions. Not selling anything. Could I ask you 3 questions about your experience? 15 minutes.

**Follow-up email**
> Subject: Quick question about EU customer questionnaires
>
> Hi {name},
> I'm researching how AI recruiting vendors respond when EU customers ask about the AI Act. I'm not selling anything and have no product to pitch.
> Have you received a questionnaire like that? If so, I'd value 15-20 minutes to hear how it went: who answered, how long it took, and whether it affected the deal.
> Happy to share what I learn from the other founders I speak to.
> Thanks, {your name}

**After a real interview**
> Thanks again. You mentioned {specific pain}. If useful, I can run your answers through a tool I'm testing and show the draft. No obligation.

## Rules
- Ask about the past, not hypotheticals.
- Never claim the tool gives legal advice or compliance.
- Do not send a vendor's real documents to any external service without their written OK.
- Stop rule: fewer than 5 of 15 faced a real questionnaire, or nobody spent time or money.
