# Human Oversight

## Recruiter stays in control
Acme Talent AI never rejects a candidate automatically. Every rejection requires a recruiter to click a confirmation button. The ranked list is advisory only.

## Override and review
Recruiters can reorder the ranking, restore any candidate the model ranked low, and add manual notes. Each candidate card shows the top factors behind the score and a confidence indicator, so recruiters can interpret the output and spot cases where the model may be wrong.

## Customer training
During onboarding we run a 45-minute session for each customer's recruiters. It explains what the score means, the known limitations of the model, the risk of automation bias, and how to override results. A written user guide with the same content is provided in the product.
