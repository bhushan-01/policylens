# Acme Talent AI — Product Overview (FICTIONAL SAMPLE COMPANY)

## What the product does
Acme Talent AI is a CV screening and candidate ranking tool for recruiters. It reads job applications, extracts skills and experience, and ranks applicants against a job description. Recruiters see a ranked shortlist with a short written summary for each candidate.

## Intended purpose
The system is intended to help recruiters analyse and filter job applications and evaluate candidates for open roles. It is not intended for use in promotion, termination, or employee performance monitoring decisions.

## Technology
A gradient-boosted ranking model scores candidates on skills match and experience. A third-party general-purpose language model generates the written candidate summaries. Protected attributes such as gender, age, ethnicity, and photos are removed before scoring.

## Customers and market
Customers are mid-size employers and staffing agencies in Germany, France, the Netherlands and the US. The system is placed on the EU market under the Acme Talent AI brand.
