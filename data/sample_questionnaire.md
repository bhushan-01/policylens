# Sample buyer AI Act questionnaire (FICTIONAL)

1. Is your AI system classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act? Please explain the basis.
2. What is the intended purpose of the system, and what uses are explicitly excluded?
3. How do you ensure effective human oversight of the system's outputs?
4. Does the system automatically log events, and for how long are logs retained?
5. Describe how training data is governed and how you test for bias.
6. What accuracy metrics do you declare, and how are they measured?
7. What cybersecurity measures protect the system?
8. Do you maintain technical documentation as required by Annex IV? Can you share it?
9. What is the status of your conformity assessment and CE marking?
10. Describe your procedure for reporting serious incidents to authorities.
11. How do you monitor the system's performance after deployment?
12. Is the system registered in the EU database for high-risk AI systems?
13. Can our recruiters explain a ranking decision to an affected candidate?
14. Does the system infer candidates' emotions or use biometric data?
